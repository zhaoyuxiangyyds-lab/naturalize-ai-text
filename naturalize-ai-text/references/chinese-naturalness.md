## 1. Scope

Use this reference after `composition-and-reconstruction.md` and the selected genre guide for Simplified or Traditional Chinese. It governs Chinese realization of a content-led draft; it is not a Tencent rule, a phrase blacklist, or a proven detector recipe.

Do not manufacture a supposedly human Chinese style. Naturalness may be concise, formal, technical, literary, regional, conversational, or deliberately regular. Derive register and variation from audience, genre, approved samples, and the material itself.

## 2. Chinese discourse decisions

### Topic and information flow

Track the active topic through each paragraph. Omit a subject only when its referent remains unique; repeat the noun when `这`, `其`, `该`, `它`, `前者`, `后者`, or a zero subject would compete with another referent. Place known and new information where the reader can resolve it without rereading.

Use a condition, object, action, observation, definition, or judgment as an opening only when it is the true local anchor. Do not rotate paragraph openings for variety. Keep explicit causal, conditional, concessive, temporal, and exception markers whenever the relation is not safely inferable; remove a connector only when the relation remains clear through shared nouns, chronology, or causality.

### Scope and modifier control

Check what every `的`, prepositional phrase, long nominal modifier, negation, modal, and quantifier governs. Split a long modifier when its attachment becomes ambiguous, not to hit a length target. Keep conditions and exceptions next to the claim they limit.

### Lexicon and register

Repeat the correct technical or thematic term. Do not cycle through near-synonyms to create lexical diversity. Use idioms, four-character phrases, internet language, dialect, particles, rhetorical questions, fragments, and sentence-final emphasis only when supported by voice, audience, or scene. Avoid importing generic elevated phrases merely to signal literary quality.

### Paragraph movement

Let paragraphs perform different work because the content differs: establish, observe, compare, infer, qualify, decide, act, remember, or change a scene. Do not require a topic sentence, example, explanation, and recap in every paragraph. Unequal paragraph length must follow unequal work, not deliberate visual irregularity.

For Chinese non-fiction and non-narrative prose, realize each traced functional unit from its committed topic and information state. Before drafting, identify the active referent and the new information the unit is allowed to introduce. After drafting, update what the reader can now infer, which term or object remains active, and which condition or uncertainty still governs the next unit. Do not draft the remaining document while completing this check. For Chinese fiction, use the continuous-scene-span route below instead of repeating this check after every beat or paragraph.

During the final read, inspect repeated rhetorical packaging rather than banning individual phrases. Constructions such as `关键不在于……而在于……`, `这也解释了为什么……`, `由此可见……`, `值得注意的是……`, or `总的来说……` are acceptable when they express a real local relation. Revise them when several units use the same construction to announce relations already clear from shared nouns, causality, or sequence. Do not replace them from a phrase list; either make the underlying relation do the work or keep the marker needed for accuracy.

### Chinese explanatory route

For an explanation, select a governing relation that already exists in the
material: an observation demanding explanation, a causal sequence, a comparison
that isolates a condition, a misconception, a temporal process, or a practical
decision. When a fact or condition overturns the reader's likely prediction or
changes what follows, use it as the governing hinge. Do not force every
explanation through the same hinge. Do not turn a source's factor list into one equally weighted paragraph per factor.

Before drafting, write a private route in terms of functions rather than
headings. Name the governing relation, the smallest mechanism the reader needs,
and the conditions, comparisons, or consequences that genuinely change it.
This is a planning aid, not a required visible sequence. Begin and end where the
material, audience, and destination warrant; keep the governing relation
recoverable throughout the piece.

When a secondary condition matters, attach it to the claim it changes instead
of postponing it to a final list of exceptions. Carry one source-supported
comparison or example through the mechanism when it clarifies the point; omit
examples that only make coverage look complete. Stop when the reader can use
the explanation, qualify it, or recognize its failure case. Do not add a
ceremonial recap merely because the source had one.

## 3. New generation

Before writing Chinese sentences:

1. Freeze script, regional usage, register, terms, names, citations, and disclosure.
2. Build the material board and select what this reader actually needs.
3. Choose a document movement from evidence, causality, time, decision, or scene pressure.
4. Decide the active topic and new information for the next functional section.
5. Draft progressively, carrying forward referents, terms, stance, and unresolved questions.

Do not first produce a complete generic Chinese answer. Do not ask the model to add human traces, colloquial words, short sentences, emotional remarks, or irregular paragraphs after the fact. Any local roughness that survives must already belong to the intended voice or function.

## 4. Full reconstruction

Extract Chinese-specific obligations into the semantic ledger:

- negation, modality, quantity, comparison basis, time, cause, condition, and exception;
- referents and topic chains;
- citation attachment and quotation boundaries;
- stable terminology, names, units, and Simplified or Traditional forms;
- narrator distance, character diction, level of formality, and supported recurring phrases.

Draft from the ledger without preserving source sentence order. Rebuild paragraph boundaries and syntax around the new document route. Preserve exact quotations, formulas, commands, citations, and required terms. After drafting, reconcile every ledger unit and inspect missing subjects, competing referents, modifier scope, and accidental changes in certainty.

Use a Chinese topic-and-information snapshot as the `state_before` and `state_change` of each traced unit. This keeps subject omission, referent choice, explanation depth, and transition use responsive to what has actually been established instead of to a prewritten smooth answer.

Do not call shuffled sentences, reordered clauses, synonym replacement, or punctuation changes a reconstruction.

## 5. Genre routes

Select only the questions relevant to the current material. Do not instantiate every item as a visible section.

### Fiction and narrative

Generate from a Chinese viewpoint and topic chain. Before choosing how the supplied event unfolds, establish a viewpoint substrate: an existing routine or relationship, a stable field of attention, ordinary unfinished business, the character's register, and what this person would leave unnamed or unexplained. Do not derive all of these from the prompt's central object, and do not invent biography or hardship to weight the choice. Before prose, commit only the character's present place and time, immediate aim or pressure, available knowledge, active uncertainty, continuity boundaries, live substrate, allowed material, and supplied ownership or custody relations. Draft a continuous scene span without pausing after each sentence, event, exchange, or paragraph to assign a function. Let several connected actions, perceptions, dialogue turns, pauses, and shifts of attention develop inside the same passage; only after the span should the trace update knowledge, pressure, relationship, risk, available action, and the active Chinese referent or omitted subject. Do not privately finish the whole moral arc, decisive choice, consequences, or ending in advance unless those are locked story facts. Do not use invented urgency, hardship, illness, children, institutional deadlines, or public duty to decide which side deserves the outcome when the source leaves comparative weight open. Ordinary handling of the place, task, or relationship may remain when it belongs to the viewpoint even if it has no later payoff, but never add filler, random sensory texture, deliberate inefficiency, or detector-facing disturbance. Differentiate speech through goals, social relation, vocabulary, pauses, evasions, interruption, and incomplete answers rather than through assigned catchphrases or a perfectly ordered interrogation of the premise.

For revision, first decide whether the existing event chain, viewpoint movement, character pressure, and viewpoint substrate already work. Preserve them and repair bounded narration, dialogue, or viewpoint defects when they do. For full reconstruction, convert only locked plot facts, scene conditions, knowledge boundaries, ownership, custody, comparative stakes, and supported voice evidence into a story ledger; do not turn the source's already-resolved theme into a mandatory new outline unless the user locked it. Reconstruct in continuous scene spans rather than preserving source paragraphs or allocating conflict, revelation, choice, consequence, and closure across tidy micro-units. Reject prompt capture when nearly every paragraph, prop, weather detail, memory, and exchange exists to explain or settle the supplied dilemma; strengthen the character's independently motivated life rather than inserting random digressions. Audit whether the ending merely brings back the opening object, date, weather, sound, or gesture to create a neat emotional picture after the scene has already stopped. Reject viewpoint leaks, changed chronology, arbitrary ambiguity, invented moral weighting, over-explained emotion, identical character registers, compulsory escalation, universal moralizing, or an aphoristic ending unsupported by the genre.

### Explanation and popular science

Choose the reader's actual question or observation and the minimum model that
answers it. Identify the material's hinge: the fact or condition that makes a
common prediction fail or changes what the reader should do. Let that hinge
govern emphasis and grouping. Develop mechanism, boundary, exception, or
practical consequence only to the depth the topic requires; do not give every
factor equal space simply because it appears in the source.

For reconstruction, map definitions, causal steps, operating conditions, limits,
and advice before rebuilding. Quarantine the source's list order and rebuild
around the hinge and its causal consequences. Keep a condition beside the
claim it limits, and use one complete source-supported comparison when it
clarifies the mechanism. Reject changed scope, anthropomorphic falsehood,
misplaced exceptions, ornamental synonym variation, a fixed
`定义-因素-例子-总结` sequence, one-paragraph-per-factor scaffolding, or advice
that outruns the evidence.

### Commentary and argument

Separate fact, evaluation criterion, judgment, forecast, risk, and action. Start from the live dispute, decision, or tradeoff. Select evidence because it can change the judgment, not because a balanced outline needs another point. Address a serious objection when it affects the conclusion; do not insert ceremonial balance. Let each unit answer the argument's current unresolved pressure rather than following a precommitted `立论-理由-反方-总结` sequence.

For reconstruction, build the new route around the real dispute or decision rather than the source headings. Stop when the judgment has a defensible scope or usable consequence. Reject public-consensus filler, automatic ordinal lists, weakened opposing views, a compulsory counterargument, or a conclusion that merely repeats and uplifts.

### Reports

First identify the report's operating job: record status, explain an incident, evaluate options, request a decision, assign work, or define a service boundary. Keep decision state, owner, threshold, dependency, deadline, exception path, and completion evidence explicit only where the task supplies or requires them. Organize around the decision bottleneck, exception, or handoff that controls the reader's next action; do not turn the report into a position essay.

For revision, preserve a working field order, service interface, or compliance structure. Reconstruct only when the report's governing decision route is defective, not because its language is regular. Reject hidden ownership, changed thresholds, invented commitments, decorative pros and cons, repeated benefit claims, or an uplift ending.

### Academic and technical prose

Preserve conventional section order, definitions before necessary use, method and result sequence, citation proximity, formulas, tables, commands, specifications, and reproducibility. Naturalize only eligible prose. Precision and stable repetition outrank stylistic variety.

For reconstruction, reorganize reasoning inside permitted prose sections but do not move a limitation away from its claim or alter evidence strength. Reject colloquialization, term rotation, causal overclaiming, citation drift, decorative examples, or removal of required signposting.

### Literary essay and memoir

Build from supplied objects, actions, places, remembered fragments, uncertainty, and reflection. Let a governing image develop without explaining it every time. A plain sentence or local repetition may remain when it belongs to the approved voice.

For reconstruction, preserve the source's factual and uncertain memory units before changing form. Reject invented biography, emotion, location, dialogue, polished sensory detail, compulsory life lessons, or a climb from every private object to society and the age.

### Speech

Write for a known audience, occasion, duration, stake, commitment, and requested action. Shape clauses for speaking and hearing; use repetition only when it aids memory, emphasis, response, or ceremonial form.

For reconstruction, preserve forms of address, commitments, sensitive facts, and timing while rebuilding speakable units. Reject false shared experience, slogan chains, decorative call-and-response, altered commitments, or an ending with no concrete audience action when one is required.

## 6. Surface and integrity review

Review separately for:

- missing, duplicated, or near-form characters and homophone substitutions;
- Simplified and Traditional consistency, regional terminology, names, numbers, dates, and units;
- subject and pronoun reference, modifier scope, negation, modality, and comparison targets;
- paired punctuation, quotation levels, title marks, list punctuation, and full-width conventions;
- citation boundaries, quotations, formulas, identifiers, and required formatting;
- unintended repetition versus functional recurrence;
- paragraph or sentence forms that recur without a shared function.

Read once without looking at any detector result. Fix reader problems; do not change correct Chinese merely because a metric looks regular.

## 7. Reject conditions

Reject a Chinese candidate when it:

- applies sentence-length ratios, paragraph quotas, transition limits, punctuation targets, idiom counts, synonym-diversity targets, or colloquialism counts;
- forces every paragraph to start concretely or end incompletely;
- replaces one templated structure with another;
- introduces unsupported personal detail, emotion, examples, data, or citations;
- changes causality, negation, modality, quantity, uncertainty, chronology, viewpoint, ownership, or conclusion strength;
- reads as patched sentence variants rather than one document;
- sacrifices grammar, spelling, terminology, logic, continuity, disclosure, or normal Chinese quality for a detector score.
