## 1. Scope

Use this reference after `composition-and-reconstruction.md` and the selected genre guide for English prose. English is not one voice. Preserve the requested dialect, house style, register, audience, and genre. Possible detector benefit is unverified until the exact text is tested.

Do not manufacture informality, contractions, fragments, first-person asides, or rare words. Do not alternate sentence lengths or syntax according to a recipe.

## 2. Information and clause decisions

Give each clause a visible relation to the current claim, event, or question. Choose subjects and verbs that expose agency when agency matters; retain passives when the actor is unknown, irrelevant, or conventionally backgrounded. Keep qualifications near the propositions they limit.

Use transitions for real turns. When a repeated noun, chronology, contrast, or causal relation already guides the reader, an added discourse marker may be unnecessary. When the inference is not safe, state it. Do not remove signposting solely to make prose less predictable.

Realize each traced functional unit from its committed subject, claim, and information state. Before drafting, identify what the reader already has, which proposition or event this unit may add, and what qualification must travel with it. After drafting, update the established claim, live question, or story pressure before selecting the next unit. Do not draft the rest of the document during that update.

In the final read, audit repeated premise packaging rather than blacklisting phrases. Frames such as `The key is`, `This explains why`, `In that role`, `It is important to note`, or a sequence of rhetorical questions may be exact and useful once. Revise them when several units use the same frame to announce relations the content already carries. Repair the underlying selection or relation instead of swapping in a synonym.

Prefer established collocations and stable terminology over thesaurus variation. Use nominalization when it names a stable technical concept; unpack it when it conceals action or responsibility. Preserve deliberate anaphora, refrain, parallel syntax, and key-term repetition.

## 3. New generation

Select and prioritize material before drafting. Decide the reader problem, stance or scene pressure, evidence boundary, and intended movement. Draft progressively from the material board, carrying forward terms, claims, motives, and unresolved questions.

Use the bounded composition trace. Commit one unit's material and job, draft that unit, record its actual reader, argument, or story-state change, and only then choose the next unit.

Let functional differences produce local form. A source limitation may require a qualified sentence; a decision may need a direct one; narrative action may compress while reflection expands. Do not create these differences to satisfy a burstiness or perplexity target.

Avoid global polishing passes that make every paragraph equally complete and every transition equally explicit. Preserve supported local emphasis and stop when the genre's work is done.

## 4. Full reconstruction

Do not enter this route from provenance alone. First inventory the source's governing movement, paragraph jobs, evidence relations, local emphasis, voice, genre function, and ending. If those decisions already work and defects are bounded, preserve them through local editing or return the exact source unchanged. An exact favorable detector observation belongs only to that hash; do not erase it with an unnecessary rewrite.

Extract claims, evidence, qualifications, citation attachment, terminology, stance, chronology, narrative state, and voice traits before drafting. Then write from the ledger without preserving source sentence or paragraph correspondence.

Rebuild the argument, explanation, report, or scene around its real relations. Restore exact quotations, citations, formulas, names, and required terms during reconciliation. Check every candidate claim against the ledger and every ledger unit against the candidate.

Commit ledger IDs before each reconstructed unit and record the resulting state afterward. A trace that follows source paragraph numbers in order is evidence that the old scaffold was not actually quarantined.

Do not use sentence-by-sentence paraphrase, synonym churn, contraction insertion, transition deletion, clause splitting, or punctuation variation as a substitute for reconstruction.

## 5. Genre-sensitive checks

### Fiction and narrative

Track viewpoint, knowledge, time, setting, motivation, and changed options. For new fiction, establish the present pressure without prewriting the final choice, consequence, theme, or ending; choose each next event from the state produced by the last. Let diction and conversational behavior follow character goals and relationships. Reject narrator explanations that duplicate enacted meaning, generic atmosphere, identical dialogue registers, mechanically paid-off details, and unsupported epigrammatic endings.

### Essays and memoir

Preserve sourced experience and uncertainty. Let concrete material support reflection without forcing every image into a universal claim. Reject invented memory, fashionable metaphor, false intimacy, and decorative specificity.

### Academic and technical writing

Preserve definitions, methods, results, limitations, citation scope, terminology, units, formulas, and conventional organization. Regular language may be correct and necessary. Reject detector-driven informality, unstable terms, shortened qualifications, citation drift, and causal inflation.

### Reports

Preserve a working report interface. Make only the supplied decision state, evidence, risk, owner, threshold, dependency, date, and completion criterion usable, organized around the decision or exception that matters next. Reject elegant ambiguity, hidden agency, generic benefit claims, invented commitments, and synonym variation in defined fields.

### Explanations

Choose a usable model and develop only the necessary mechanism, conditions, boundaries, and consequences. Reject definition-list-summary automation, disconnected examples, anthropomorphic falsehood, and repeated recap.

### Commentary and speeches

For commentary, start from the live dispute or tradeoff and let each unit address the current unresolved pressure; do not precommit a support-objection-summary route. Ground stance in a real criterion and distinguish facts, values, forecasts, and recommendations. For speech, make units speakable and preserve the actual audience relationship and commitments. Reject ceremonial balance, slogan triads, false shared experience, and uplift that replaces a decision or action.

## 6. Usage review

Choose US, UK, or supplied house usage and check it consistently. Review articles, prepositions, agreement, tense sequence, pronoun reference, modifier attachment, punctuation, capitalization, quotation style, and spelling separately from document movement.

Read once for meaning and once aloud where genre permits. A fluent surface cannot repair a missing premise, unsupported claim, broken viewpoint, or citation error.

## 7. Reject conditions

Reject an English candidate when it:

- globally rewrites a working source solely because it was AI-generated or AI-assisted;
- uses fixed sentence ratios, vocabulary rarity targets, transition quotas, contraction quotas, or punctuation recipes;
- substitutes supposedly human idioms, fragments, or asides without voice support;
- preserves the source macrostructure under local paraphrase;
- changes facts, qualifications, causality, chronology, viewpoint, citation scope, terminology, or disclosure;
- creates deliberate awkwardness or errors for a score;
- can justify its revisions only by a local style statistic rather than by reader, evidence, genre, or voice needs.
