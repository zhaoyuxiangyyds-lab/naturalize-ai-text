## 1. General rules

Freeze facts, numbers, quotations, sources, chronology, relationships, and task constraints before writing. Apply the evidence rules of the highest-risk component in mixed work. Make every material change serve content, evidence, logic, voice, genre, or reader use. Do not retain a quality-negative change merely because a detector score improved.

For `new_generation`, select and prioritize material before drafting from the claim or story ledger. For revision, run the preservation and scaffold diagnosis in `composition-and-reconstruction.md`; do not infer depth from AI provenance. Preserve or edit locally when the source's genre movement already works. Reconstruct only when a named scaffold defect requires a new movement or an authorized blank-page request remains appropriate.

The prompts below are decision aids, not ingredients. Select only those required by the current material. Do not force a complete set, a fixed order, a fixed number of beats, or one genre's variation pattern onto another. Regularity can be functional in methods, specifications, oral refrains, or verse. Follow `composition-and-reconstruction.md` for the writing mode and the language-specific reference for realization.

Use the bounded composition trace with a genre-specific state. This table identifies what a functional unit may change; it is not a mandatory visible outline:

| Genre | State carried between units |
|---|---|
| Fiction and narrative | viewpoint knowledge, desire, pressure, available choices, relationship, risk, and unresolved consequence |
| Literary essay and memoir | sourced object or episode, memory certainty, association, reflective tension, and what remains unclaimed |
| Research and academic | question, claim, evidence attached, method or result status, limitation, and permissible inference |
| Technical and business report | decision state, evidence, owner, threshold, dependency, exception path, and completion condition |
| Explanation and popular science | reader's current model, causal step established, operating condition, failure case, and usable consequence |
| Commentary and argument | contested claim, evaluation criterion, evidence weight, live objection, judgment scope, and action |
| Speech | audience knowledge, stake, authorized commitment, requested action, and what must be memorable when heard |
| Classical poetry and couplets | source boundary, image or action state, formal constraint, semantic progression, and unresolved implication |

Before drafting a unit, commit the material IDs and the state change it is meant to perform. After drafting, record what it actually changed. Let the next unit respond to that updated genre state. Do not use the same `premise-three points-objection-summary` state machine across genres, and do not invent changes merely to make a trace look dynamic.

## 2. Fiction and narrative

### Protect

Plot facts, character goals, knowledge boundaries, viewpoint, chronology, world rules, foreshadowing, relationships, and author-defined voice.

### Diagnose

- emotion explained before it is enacted;
- identical diction and sentence completeness across characters;
- generic atmosphere such as darkness, silence, or tension without a viewpoint-specific observation;
- even paragraphs and predictable scene endings;
- every conflict immediately interpreted for the reader;
- the prompt's central object or dilemma consumes the character's entire attention, history, vocabulary, and social world;
- every secondary character exists only to state one side of the supplied choice or ask the next necessary expository question;
- stock promises such as "the real challenge had only begun."

### Generate

- before prose, establish only the viewpoint character's present place and time, available knowledge, immediate aim or pressure, active uncertainty, and continuity boundaries;
- before choosing the event route in Chinese fiction, establish a viewpoint substrate from an existing routine, relationship, attention field, register, and unfinished ordinary concern; do not derive all of them from the inciting object;
- do not pre-solve the character's final decision, complete consequence, theme, moral choice, or ending unless the user supplied it as a locked plot fact;
- begin where the viewpoint character's current situation can be inhabited; pressure may be active, latent, or arrive during the span, so do not require an opening anomaly or immediate conflict;
- let the viewpoint determine which concrete details enter and which remain unnoticed;
- for Chinese fiction and narrative, commit and draft a continuous scene span that may include several connected events, exchanges, pauses, and paragraphs; do not stop after each microbeat to optimize its function;
- within that Chinese route, allow ordinary handling of the place, task, or relationship when it belongs to the moment, without requiring every object or gesture to become a clue, symbol, reversal, or later payoff;
- let the supplied dilemma interrupt or coexist with ongoing life. Reject a draft in which nearly every passage is one more step in restating, weighing, or settling the brief;
- give secondary characters local work and conversational aims beyond representing an option. Let necessary facts emerge without turning dialogue into a perfectly sequenced interview;
- preserve supplied ownership, custody, evidence, entitlement, and uncertainty; do not turn `unclaimed`, `unknown`, `possible`, `prepaid`, or another exact relation into a stronger convenient backstory;
- keep competing stakes open unless the authoritative material weights them; do not invent a high-sympathy deadline, illness, child, financial hardship, or public obligation to make one choice automatically righteous;
- after the Chinese scene span, update knowledge, pressure, relationship, risk, and available actions, then decide whether another span is needed;
- for other languages, retain the language-specific drafting process and do not import the Chinese scene-span intervention merely to create variation;
- let decisions and consequences emerge from accumulated scene conditions; complete the requested narrative obligation without preselecting a resonant object, moral sentence, tightly mirrored closure, or preservation-for-later-revelation ending;
- carry tension through action, inference, dialogue, and changed options instead of explanatory summaries;
- use approved human or AI reference passages to set the target voice, while keeping identity and autobiographical claims separate.

For an authorized retry after a failed exact-hash candidate, return to the raw brief and declared fictional frame. Do not read prior candidate prose or carry forward its invented event chain, choice, motif system, emotional explanation, or ending. Those elements are not locked merely because an earlier candidate used them. Build a material-only story ledger containing the supplied situation, viewpoint boundary, pressures, required decision, chronology, and open possibilities. Let the new event chain emerge from that state. When the brief supplies scarcity or competing claims, do not silently treat a fair allocation by the viewpoint character as a locked plot. The decision owner, alternatives, selected action, beneficiary, cost bearer, knowledge change, and object or relationship state remain open unless the brief fixes them. Do not deliberately reverse the old choice or relocate its symbols; inversion still copies the failed design.

After provisional freeze, compare each control and the candidate by title concept, initiating pressure, decision owner, alternatives, selected action or resource disposition, immediate beneficiary and cost bearer, irreversible consequence, viewpoint knowledge change, final object or relationship state, and closure job. Mark which fields the brief forced. Reject recurrence only when an unlocked combination substantially recreates the same event-choice-consequence-close movement; a shared setting, name, required object, or other locked fact is not enough. A rejection requires a fresh context and an unused positively justified route option from the pre-prose register, not an edited or inverted version of the exposed story.

### Revise

- diagnose whether the existing event chain and viewpoint movement already work before rebuilding the plot; retain them when only local narration or dialogue is defective;
- express emotion through choice, action, bodily constraint, and consequence;
- release information through conflict and viewpoint rather than narrator explanation;
- give characters distinct vocabulary, pauses, evasions, and conversational goals;
- select sensory detail that the viewpoint character would notice now;
- allow silence, interruption, fragments, and misunderstanding when motivated;
- delete summaries of what the scene already demonstrated.

### Validate

Check viewpoint leaks, time-space continuity, motives, clue placement, repeated gestures, and character-specific speech. In Chinese fiction, verify across a scene or scene sequence that story state eventually changes; do not require every paragraph, gesture, or exchange to advertise a distinct function. Run the prompt-capture audit from `composition-and-reconstruction.md`: confirm that the viewpoint still has recognizable language, relations, attention, and unfinished life when summarized without the central object. Audit every invented fact that changes comparative moral weight and every change to ownership, custody, or evidence. Read the ending once without the last sentence or paragraph; if the story already stops and the remainder mainly re-presents the opening object, date, weather, sound, or gesture as emotional residue, remove the engineered tail unless a locked consequence requires it. In every language, reject prose whose microbeats visibly execute a preallocated conflict-revelation-choice-consequence-close chain, or whose final sentence explains what the events already mean. Ordinary scene business must arise from viewpoint and setting, not from random texture, deliberate inefficiency, filler, or detector targeting.

Example:

> Before: Lin Lan felt very nervous. Darkness covered the street, and she knew the real test had begun.
>
> After: The key turned twice and stopped. On the third floor, a chair scraped across tile. Lin Lan pulled the key out; her palm had warmed the metal.

## 3. Literary essays and memoir

### Protect

The author's real experiences, locations, dates, relationships, quotations, uncertainty of memory, and core stance. Never manufacture childhood, trauma, family detail, or first-person observation.

### Diagnose

- public metaphors such as time leaving traces or home being a harbor;
- dense abstract nouns and repeated "not X but Y" constructions;
- every image explained after it appears;
- paragraph-by-paragraph elevation toward life, civilization, or the age;
- details too polished to be traceable to the author's notes.

### Generate

- select only source-grounded objects, actions, places, remembered fragments, and uncertainty;
- decide what the present reflection is trying to understand without requiring a universal lesson;
- let emphasis follow the supplied material, including silence or incomplete memory;
- never create autobiographical detail merely to make the voice seem personal.

### Revise

- center each passage on a small number of physical objects, actions, or sounds;
- derive unusual detail from source notes, not invention;
- preserve uncertainty, partial memory, bias, and silence when authentic;
- limit governing metaphors and stop when the image has completed the thought;
- allow a plain sentence or local repetition when it belongs to the writer's voice.

### Validate

Verify every first-person claim. Check geography, dialect, season, material culture, and period. Remove unsupported attractive detail.

## 4. Research and academic writing

### Protect

Research question, sample, data, method, definitions, statistical results, limitations, cited meaning, source identity, citation style, and causal direction.

### Diagnose

- "many studies show" without sources;
- background-status-significance templates that delay the research problem;
- correlation rewritten as causation;
- the same conclusion repeated with synonyms;
- fabricated or mismatched references, pages, or identifiers;
- limitations reduced to a ceremonial final paragraph;
- uniform topic sentences that hide different evidentiary functions.

### Generate

- state the research question, evidence boundary, and permissible conclusion before drafting;
- assign each paragraph one evidentiary action and connect it to a named source or result;
- introduce definitions and limitations where they first constrain interpretation;
- keep methods and results regular when reproducibility requires regularity.

### Revise

- give each paragraph one argument action: define, compare, establish evidence, test, qualify, or infer;
- separate fact, method, result, interpretation, and recommendation;
- replace vague magnitude words with sample, effect, interval, range, or condition;
- place limits next to the claim they constrain;
- vary paragraph form only when evidence type differs;
- retain standardized and predictable language when precision requires it.

### Validate

Build a claim-evidence-source table. Verify names, dates, pages, DOIs, sample size, units, formulas, statistical direction, and quotation text. A reader should be able to state what was done, found, and not established.

Never alter terminology, data precision, citation form, or conclusion strength merely to change a detector score.

## 5. Technical and business reports

### Protect

Decision state, requirements, interfaces, metrics, thresholds, owners, dates, dependencies, cost, risk, and compliance constraints. Keep proprietary terms consistent.

### Diagnose

- empty verbs such as enable, empower, optimize, and form a closed loop;
- agentless sentences that obscure ownership;
- mechanical three-part lists;
- facts, risks, recommendations, and decisions mixed together;
- actions without an owner, trigger, deadline, or completion criterion.

### Generate

- decide whether the document records status, explains an incident, evaluates options, requests a decision, assigns work, or defines an operating boundary;
- identify the one decision, exception, bottleneck, or handoff that controls what the reader must do; let required fields attach to that need rather than forming an essay arc;
- build only the sections required by decision state, evidence, risk, action, owner, and completion criterion;
- keep required headings and repeated field names stable;
- separate prose naturalization from tables, commands, identifiers, and compliance language that must remain exact.

### Revise

- lead with the decision or problem, then evidence;
- state who does what by when and to what observable threshold;
- separate fact, judgment, risk, and action;
- replace "timely" and "effective" with events and limits;
- retain necessary terms and delete synonym packaging.
- preserve a working report interface and field order; do not turn an operational report into commentary merely to vary its structure.

### Validate

Check executability, units, time zones, versions, owners, deadlines, exception paths, and agreement among summary, tables, and body. Do not invent metrics or commitments.

## 6. Explanatory and popular science writing

### Protect

Definitions, mechanisms, conditions, consensus, dispute status, exceptions, risks, and audience level. A metaphor cannot replace the mechanism.

### Diagnose

- definition followed by three generic features and a statement of importance;
- one paragraph per source factor, followed by a complete advice recap;
- benefits without operating conditions;
- anthropomorphic mechanisms that become false;
- multiple near-synonyms for one technical concept;
- examples harder than the principle.

### Generate

- select a governing relation from the actual material: observation, causal
  sequence, comparison, misconception, temporal process, or practical decision;
  when a condition makes the common prediction fail or changes the useful
  action, let that condition become the hinge rather than another list item;
- specify the smallest usable model and causal chain before drafting; attach
  operating conditions and failure cases where they change that chain rather
  than presenting them as an inventory;
- carry one example through the whole mechanism instead of adding disconnected mini-examples;
- choose terminology once and repeat it accurately rather than cycling through synonyms.

### Revise

- rebuild factor-by-factor source scaffolds around a governing relation chosen
  from the material, not a universal explanation template;
- provide a usable model at the point the reader needs it, then follow the
  mechanism through the condition that changes the answer;
- state a metaphor's boundary immediately;
- carry one example through the causal chain;
- distinguish usually, possibly, necessarily, and sufficiently.

### Validate

An intended reader should answer: what is happening, how does it work, which
condition changes the answer, and what follows from that change? Verify term
consistency, scope, and dispute status. Reject a candidate whose document route
can be described only as a complete list of the source's factors.

## 7. Commentary and argument

### Protect

Factual premises, quoted positions, evaluation criteria, and the author's actual value stance.

### Diagnose

- a safe consensus instead of a contestable claim;
- evidence listed without a stated evaluation rule;
- weak objections substituted for the strongest counterargument;
- factual, value, and predictive claims blended together;
- a conclusion that repeats the introduction and adds uplift.

### Generate

- locate the live dispute, decision, or tradeoff before choosing a route; state the evaluation criterion only as explicitly as the audience needs;
- select evidence by what can change the judgment, not by filling supporting slots; test a serious objection or counterexample only when it bears on the actual conclusion;
- separate factual premises, value judgments, forecasts, and recommendations;
- do not precommit a `claim-support-objection-summary` sequence; let the next unit answer the current unresolved pressure in the argument;
- stop when the judgment is sufficiently bounded for the reader to decide or act; do not add a restatement or uplift paragraph.

For an authorized retry after a failed exact-hash candidate, extract propositions, criteria, risks, thresholds, qualifications, and recommendations from the authoritative source, verify them, and then close both the source wording and all failed candidate prose. Choose a governing dispute or decision from the ledger rather than preserving or reversing the earlier paragraph sequence. Do not reproduce a balanced point-by-point march merely with different transitions, and do not invent a case, survey, stakeholder voice, or institutional fact to make the argument look situated. After freeze, audit the opening anchor, governing criterion, evidence grouping, objection placement, action or threshold, and closing implication. Distinguish source-required relations from an unlocked sequence; wording independence does not cure a repeated argument movement.

### Revise

- state the contestable judgment and its criterion;
- organize evidence by relevance or strength;
- address the strongest reasonable counterexample or alternative explanation;
- label prediction and uncertainty;
- end by refining the judgment, drawing a bounded implication, or proposing an action.

### Validate

Check whether the conclusion would change if a key premise failed. Preserve fair characterization of opposing views and cite disputed facts.

## 8. Speeches

### Protect

Speaker identity, audience, occasion, duration, authorized commitments, sensitive facts, forms of address, and requested action.

### Diagnose

- "an era of opportunities and challenges" openings;
- repeated three-slogan sequences;
- written sentences too long to speak;
- ambition replacing a concrete action;
- endings composed only of working together and creating a better future.

### Generate

- identify the actual audience, occasion, time limit, stake, authorized commitment, and requested action;
- select one or more verified shared reference points only when they genuinely belong to the occasion;
- draft for listening, carrying the audience through the necessary turn rather than filling a slogan structure;
- use repetition only when it performs memory, emphasis, response, or ceremonial work.

### Revise

- open with a shared verified fact, real problem, or decision;
- write in breath-length units;
- use repetition only for memory, emphasis, or audience response;
- place short sentences and pauses before key turns;
- end with one action the audience can take.

### Validate

Read aloud and time it. Mark breath points and tongue-twisters. Verify stories and commitments. Confirm that listeners can recall the core sentence and next step.

## 9. Classical Chinese poetry and couplets

### Protect

The subject, boundary between history and legend, form, length, rhyme system, tonal standard, display constraints, and taboos.

### Diagnose

- interchangeable clouds, moon, wind, lake, eternity, and poetic soul;
- dense allusions unrelated to the exact place or object;
- synonymous upper and lower lines;
- mismatched word class, syntax, rhythm, or tone;
- local tradition stated as verified history;
- a modern slogan replacing poetic movement.

### Generate

- establish the subject, source boundary, form, tonal standard, rhyme system, display constraint, and taboo before composing;
- select source-supported hard nouns, directional verbs, and allusions rather than filling a stock image inventory;
- let each line alter image, action, time, relation, or implication instead of completing a decorative checklist;
- preserve the distinction between history, tradition, reception, and invention.

### Revise

- establish a source and allusion list first;
- distinguish official history, local gazetteer, old tradition, literary reception, and present object;
- prefer one irreplaceable hard noun and one directional verb per short unit;
- let parallel structure carry semantic progression, not repetition;
- use boundary words such as tradition says, transmits, remembers, or old account when needed;
- embody public or modern meaning in a physical image rather than a slogan.

### Validate

Mark tone, word class, syntax, rhythm, repeated characters, semantic duplication, line endings, and allusion source. Read aloud and check the physical display. Exact-phrase search can flag accidental reuse but cannot prove authorship.

## 10. Mixed-genre routing

- Apply research-report evidence rules to factual exposition inside fiction or essays.
- Treat personal memory in essays as locked evidence even when the surrounding prose is literary.
- Treat cases in reports as traceable evidence; narrative craft must not change evidentiary weight.
- Keep technical terms authoritative in white papers while adapting explanations to the audience.
- Verify all statistics in speeches as research claims.
- Process a local legend in a poem as literature while preserving its historical status.
