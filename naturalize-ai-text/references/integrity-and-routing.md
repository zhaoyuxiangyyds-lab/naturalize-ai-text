# Integrity and Routing

## Purpose

Route naturalization work by provenance, context, and permission before editing. A detector score must never be used as fabricated proof that a person wrote a text.

## Provenance routes

| Route | Meaning | Voice rule |
|---|---|---|
| `human_draft` | The user says a person drafted the supplied text | Preserve correct idiosyncrasies; do not homogenize the writer |
| `ai_assisted` | Human material was substantially assisted by a model | Use it as content and voice material; disclose assistance where required |
| `ai_generated` | The draft was generated by a model | Use it as content and voice material; never relabel it as human-authored |
| `mixed` | Human and generated regions coexist | Use all approved regions; keep region-level provenance when known |
| `unknown` | Provenance cannot be established | Use the text as a reference without inferring authorship from style or a detector |

Record which drafts, samples, or notes were supplied, their provenance when known, and the role assigned to each. Human, AI-assisted, AI-generated, mixed, and unknown-origin material may all guide content, structure, style, or target voice. A sample generated inside the same task may set a provisional voice and continuity target for later passages. It is not independent evidence that a real person has that voice, lived the described events, or wrote without AI.

When detector reduction is a stated priority, route approved same-language, same-genre complete texts through [editorial-anchors.md](editorial-anchors.md). Freeze their hashes and provenance, extract decisions about selection and emphasis, and quarantine their wording during drafting. If no suitable text is supplied, record `anchor_basis: none`; do not manufacture a corpus or infer one from detector scores.

## Work modes

### Revision

Freeze the source text and locked claims. Derive the target voice from the source draft, user-approved samples of any provenance, or explicit voice constraints. Treat "author voice" as a text-level target unless a real-person authorship claim is separately supported.

Select revision depth from the text, task, and genre, not from provenance. First make a preservation inventory: governing movement, functional paragraph or scene jobs, evidence and causal relations, voice decisions, useful emphasis or recurrence, required form, and ending. Then list document-level defects and bounded defects separately.

- Choose `local_edit` when the preservation inventory is substantially usable and every authorized defect can be repaired without replacing the governing movement. This route may return the exact source unchanged when no real defect remains.
- Choose `full_reconstruction` when the governing movement is generic, incoherent, wrongly selected, or tied to sentence-by-sentence packaging strongly enough that local repair would leave the same defective scaffold. Also choose it for an explicit blank-page rewrite unless locked form or an already valid exact-text advantage makes that choice unsafe; disclose the conflict instead of silently destroying the advantage.
- Never treat `ai_generated`, `ai_assisted`, or a high detector score as a sufficient reconstruction trigger. A score identifies an exact sample for investigation; it does not diagnose which writing decision failed.

When the exact source hash already has a valid detector observation and the text passes integrity, genre, and language review, preservation is the control route. Editing produces a new hash with no inherited result. Keep the source result only as exact-sample evidence and never claim it will transfer.

### Retry after an unfavorable exact-hash observation

Treat the observation as evidence about the submitted bytes, not as a diagnosis of sentences or authorship. Record the detector, displayed version or update date, exact hash, visible components, evidence path, and same-task relationship. Then classify the prior candidate as a control:

```yaml
failed_candidate_controls:
  - candidate_id:
    text_sha256:
    observation:
    relation: same_prompt|same_source|same_task
    authoritative_source: true|false
    independently_authorized_material: []
    unavailable_during_drafting: true
retry_basis: explicit_blank_page|named_scaffold_trigger
```

Do not route to reconstruction from the detector label alone. Require either explicit blank-page recomposition authority or a document-level trigger recorded without using the score as a prose diagnosis. If neither exists, preserve the text or ask for the missing authority; do not perform detector-directed local perturbation.

Once retry is authorized, separate sources from candidates. Raw briefs, frozen source documents, citations, user constraints, approved voice evidence, and later authorized facts are authoritative material. Failed intermediate candidates are controls, even when they were fluent and quality-approved. Extract material from an original source when necessary, freeze the ledger, then make all failed-candidate prose and structure unavailable during drafting. Do not silently promote a candidate-only invention, example, event, metaphor, or conclusion into the ledger.

An unfavorable result for one exact hash does not invalidate a favorable exact hash for another sample. Preserve successful hashes and their working genre routes unless an independent factual, reader, genre, disclosure, or language defect requires change.

### New generation

Build a text contract and material board from user constraints and approved reference material of any provenance before drafting prose. Select emphasis, depth, and omissions from reader and genre needs; do not first generate a generic complete draft for later humanization. Call the result a requested or derived target voice, not restoration of a real person's voice unless that claim is independently supported. Never turn fictional or generated autobiographical material into a factual claim merely to make the text seem human.

### Experiment only

Freeze every input. Do not revise unless the user also authorized editing. Preserve all results, not only favorable ones.

## Context routes

### Private creative work

Normal generation or revision is permitted. Keep authorship claims accurate.

### Ordinary publication

Check the destination's disclosure and attribution rules when supplied or readily available. Preserve required notices and sources.

### Assessed or high-stakes submission

Ask for or inspect the governing rule when detector-directed editing could conceal prohibited assistance. Help with learning, transparent editing, citation repair, and compliant disclosure. Do not create false evidence of unaided authorship, forge process records, or claim that a detector result proves compliance.

## Permission boundaries

Detector testing can transmit text to a third party. Confirm that the text is suitable to submit, especially when it contains unpublished research, personal data, confidential business information, or copyrighted material. Respect site quotas, authentication, CAPTCHAs, and terms.

## Non-negotiable prohibitions

- fabricated identity, biography, memories, interviews, experiments, sources, data, quotations, or citations;
- false claims that the text was written without AI;
- removal or defeat of watermarks or provider-side provenance mechanisms;
- deliberate misspellings, grammar faults, homoglyphs, invisible characters, random punctuation, or encoding tricks;
- translation loops or paraphrase loops accepted without semantic and quality verification;
- unrelated padding, repeated passages, shuffled logic, weakened limitations, or altered results;
- cherry-picked detector runs or scores inherited by a different text hash.
- fixed sentence, paragraph, transition, punctuation, vocabulary, colloquialism, or rhetorical quotas used to imitate statistical variation.

## Decision record

Use this compact record:

```yaml
mode: revision|new_generation|experiment_only
revision_depth: full_reconstruction|local_edit|not_applicable
route_reason:
provenance: human_draft|ai_assisted|ai_generated|mixed|unknown
reference_material: supplied|not_supplied
reference_provenance: [human_draft|ai_assisted|ai_generated|mixed|unknown]
anchor_basis: approved_corpus|source_draft|combined|none
anchor_records: []
voice_basis: source_draft|approved_sample|explicit_constraints|combined|not_applicable
context: private_creative|ordinary_publication|assessed_or_high_stakes
disclosure_rule: known|unknown|not_applicable
detector_testing_authorized: true|false
third_party_submission_safe: true|false|unknown
assumptions: []
locked_constraints: []
locked_relations: []
```

Represent a locked relation separately from a required word. Record its subject, predicate, object when present, scope, negation, modality or certainty, complete qualifier set, and forbidden inferences. A status label does not silently establish ownership or custody; a compound constraint does not survive when only one qualifier remains. Verify these relations against the final reader-visible prose, not merely against planning notes.
