# Integrity and Routing

## Purpose

Route naturalization work by provenance, context, and permission before editing. A detector score must never be used as fabricated proof that a person wrote a text.

## Provenance routes

| Route | Meaning | Voice rule |
|---|---|---|
| `human_draft` | The user says a person drafted the supplied text | Preserve correct idiosyncrasies; do not homogenize the writer |
| `ai_assisted` | Human material was substantially assisted by a model | Preserve human-supplied content and disclose assistance where required |
| `ai_generated` | The draft was generated by a model | Improve it honestly; never relabel it as human-authored |
| `mixed` | Human and generated regions coexist | Keep region-level provenance when known; do not flatten all voices |
| `unknown` | Provenance cannot be established | Do not infer authorship from style or a detector |

Record whether an author sample or notes were supplied. A sample generated inside the same task is not independent voice evidence.

## Work modes

### Revision

Freeze the source text and locked claims. Restore voice only from the source draft, an author-approved sample, or explicit voice constraints.

### New generation

Build a text contract from user constraints. Call the result quality-first generation, not restoration of a human voice. Never invent a personal memory or lived experience to make the text seem human.

### Experiment only

Freeze every input. Do not revise unless the user also authorized editing. Preserve all results, not only favorable ones.

## Context routes

### Private creative work

Normal generation or revision is permitted. Keep authorship claims accurate.

### Ordinary publication

Check the destination's disclosure and attribution rules when supplied or readily available. Preserve required notices and sources.

### Assessed or high-stakes submission

Ask for or inspect the governing rule when detector-directed editing could conceal prohibited assistance. Help with learning, transparent editing, citation repair, and compliant disclosure. Do not create false evidence of unaided authorship, forge process records, or claim that a detector result proves compliance.

## Permission boundaries

Detector testing can transmit text to a third party. Confirm that the text is suitable to submit, especially when it contains unpublished research, personal data, confidential business information, or copyrighted material. Respect site quotas, authentication, CAPTCHAs, and terms.

## Non-negotiable prohibitions

- fabricated identity, biography, memories, interviews, experiments, sources, data, quotations, or citations;
- false claims that the text was written without AI;
- removal or defeat of watermarks or provider-side provenance mechanisms;
- deliberate misspellings, grammar faults, homoglyphs, invisible characters, random punctuation, or encoding tricks;
- translation loops or paraphrase loops accepted without semantic and quality verification;
- unrelated padding, repeated passages, shuffled logic, weakened limitations, or altered results;
- cherry-picked detector runs or scores inherited by a different text hash.

## Decision record

Use this compact record:

```yaml
mode: revision|new_generation|experiment_only
provenance: human_draft|ai_assisted|ai_generated|mixed|unknown
author_sample: supplied|not_supplied
context: private_creative|ordinary_publication|assessed_or_high_stakes
disclosure_rule: known|unknown|not_applicable
detector_testing_authorized: true|false
third_party_submission_safe: true|false|unknown
assumptions: []
locked_constraints: []
```
