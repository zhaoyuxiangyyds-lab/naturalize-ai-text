# Trace-to-Edit Map

## How to use this map

These are observable writing patterns, not proof of AI generation and not claims about a proprietary detector's internal features. Diagnose a pattern only when it creates a reader-facing defect in the target genre. Edit the cause, then recheck meaning and quality. Do not apply every row mechanically.

| Observable pattern | Reader-facing diagnosis | Supported intervention | Guard and counterexample |
|---|---|---|---|
| New wording preserves the source paragraph sequence and claim-example-recap pattern | Surface paraphrase leaves the document's original generated scaffold intact | Build a semantic ledger, design a new route, and draft from a blank page before reconciliation | Preserve methods order, chronology, citation proximity, or other relations that are genuinely locked |
| Every relevant branch receives similar space and closure | Selection is replaced by exhaustive, uniform coverage | Rank material by reader need, evidence, difficulty, decision value, or scene pressure; omit unsupported or low-value branches | Reference works and compliance documents may require exhaustive coverage |
| Every paragraph contains a complete setup, support, qualification, and recap | Local completeness produces repeated discourse shape | Give the paragraph only the work its material requires and let adjacent paragraphs share the larger movement | Standalone accessibility modules may need locally complete units |
| Local short sentences, slang, asides, or unusual punctuation appear without a voice or content reason | Added variation reads as detector-directed decoration | Remove the decoration and rebuild the underlying content or stance decision | A supplied voice, character, or oral occasion may support the same form |
| Multiple passages feel polished to the same intensity | Global smoothing erased local priority and texture | Revisit material selection and restore supported differences in depth, directness, and explanatory effort | Ceremonial, legal, and controlled technical prose may intentionally sustain one register |
| Broad context before the actual subject | Delayed entry; low information value | Start at the decision, scene, claim, or concrete question | Academic introductions may need scoped context and citations |
| Equal-length paragraphs with the same rhetorical job | Visual and functional symmetry replaces content logic | Assign distinct paragraph jobs; let evidence or scene weight determine length | Parallel legal clauses, procedures, and poems may require symmetry |
| Repeated `first/second/finally` or equivalent scaffolding | Relations are named instead of demonstrated | Reorder by cause, time, evidence strength, or decision; remove redundant labels | Instructions and safety procedures may require explicit ordering |
| Three-item lists throughout | Artificial completeness | Keep only necessary items or expand the genuinely complex branch | A real three-part taxonomy should remain three-part |
| Paragraph-end recap repeats the opening | No information gain; overly sealed paragraph | End on evidence, consequence, question, or action; delete duplicate summary | Executive summaries and accessibility contexts may need repetition |
| Conclusion restates every section and adds a moral | Over-explained closure | State the actual implication or stop after the consequence | Formal reports may require a findings summary |
| Uniformly polished transitions between every paragraph | The writer narrates relations readers can already infer | Remove or simplify transitions when local nouns, time, or causality suffice | Long technical arguments may need explicit navigation |
| Abstract praise or generic benefit language | Claims lack an object, scale, condition, or consequence | Replace with verified specifics or delete | Do not invent metrics or examples merely to add specificity |
| Every concrete detail foreshadows or pays off | Engineered economy makes a scene feel designed | Keep selective setting texture and ordinary actions that belong in the scene | Short fables may intentionally use strict economy |
| Dense ornamental specificity | Details decorate rather than constrain action or meaning | Retain details that change inference, action, mood, or character; cut the rest | Source-grounded sensory detail can be central to memoir or fiction |
| Narrator and all speakers share one register | Character goals and social relations are flattened | Differentiate diction, implication, interruption, politeness, and omissions | A deliberately formal chorus or institutional style may be uniform |
| Dialogue explains emotions or plot already visible | Subtext disappears | Let action, timing, or response carry information; keep necessary clarity | Young readers or accessibility goals may justify more explicitness |
| Every conflict resolves cleanly and every question closes | Narrative feels mechanically complete | Preserve a natural residue, cost, changed relationship, or unanswered minor thread | Do not create arbitrary ambiguity or break promised genre closure |
| Uniform emotional intensity and sentence cadence | No local emphasis; events feel equally weighted | Match rhythm to decisions, observations, reflection, and action | Minimalist or ceremonial prose may intentionally sustain a cadence |
| Repeated sentence openings or syntax | Accidental monotony | Revise only accidental repetitions; change sentence function or subject focus | Anaphora, refrain, and technical parallelism may be functional |
| Forced synonym variation | Terminology becomes unstable or ornamental | Repeat the correct key term; vary only surrounding syntax | Avoid unnecessary repetition when a pronoun or omission is clearer |
| Excessive hedging everywhere | Responsibility and evidence strength become unclear | Keep uncertainty only where evidence warrants it; state bounded claims directly | Scientific uncertainty and legal qualification must remain |
| Unqualified certainty | Evidence and stance are overstated | Separate observation, inference, and opinion; add source-backed limits | Do not invent doubt where the source is definitive |
| Exhaustive coverage with shallow treatment | Completeness crowds out priority | Select the decision-relevant or narratively consequential material | Reference documents may legitimately be exhaustive |
| Repeated generic imagery or stock emotional language | Voice becomes interchangeable | Use author-supplied or scene-supported diction; prefer precise action | Do not fabricate personal memories or eccentric phrases |
| Perfect grammar and polish | No defect by itself | Make no change solely because writing is correct | Human imperfection is not a license to insert errors |
| Platform-colored or highlighted span | The platform marked a region, but cause is unknown | Treat it as an annotation; diagnose an independent quality issue or leave it | Never infer hidden feature weights from color or CSS classes |

## New-fiction checks

For a fresh story without an author draft, scrutinize these model-common construction habits:

1. premise, rule, crisis, solution, and moral all align too perfectly;
2. three escalating attempts occur because three is conventional, not because choices differ;
3. every named prop returns as a payoff;
4. descriptive details are uniformly vivid and equally useful;
5. dialogue exists mainly to expose rules;
6. an adult or mentor delivers the final meaning;
7. the last line turns the theme into a label or aphorism.

Do not damage a good story merely because it is well made. Instead, ask whether each choice follows from character, setting, audience, and scene pressure. Prefer an authored selection of emphasis over maximal polish or random roughness.

## Factual-prose checks

Never replace standardized terminology, citations, definitions, method steps, or required headings just to change style statistics. In academic and technical prose, accurate repetition and explicit transitions may be quality-positive. The strongest naturalization often comes from a real claim ledger, source-specific limitations, and a structure that follows evidence rather than a generic template.
